HOW TO PLAYFull guide · 2 min read

THE GOAL

Pick a room, lock your entry fee and duel a real player. A match has up to 5 rounds. Win 3 rounds and you take the pot: both entry fees minus a small house fee. If the match ends level, both players get their entry back.

New here? Start with Practice vs Bot at the top of the Arena. It's free, always available, and doesn't touch your FLIP or ELO. Pick Easy, Normal or Hard.

3 · PLAY A ROUND

  1. You have 20 seconds. Tap a card in your hand, then Lock card.
  2. Your opponent does the same. Neither of you sees the other's card until both are locked.
  3. Both cards flip at the same time. The higher final power wins the round. Equal power is a tie and nobody scores.
  4. Each card can be played only once per match, so plan all 5 rounds.
  5. Run out of time and a random card from your hand is played for you.

4 · USE ELEMENTS (+30%)

FIRE›WIND›EARTH›WATER›FIRE

Each element beats the next one in the cycle: fire beats wind, wind beats earth, earth beats water, water beats fire. A card that beats its opponent's element gets +30% power. Every other pairing (fire vs earth, wind vs water, same element) is neutral.

During a match you can see the elements of your opponent's remaining cards as coloured squares under their name, but not which one they'll play. Cards in your hand show ▲ when they beat more of those elements than they lose to, and ▼ when they're at risk.

FLIP CARDS: EVERY CARD HAS TWO SIDES

Each card has a front and a back. When you pick a card in a match you also choose which side faces up. The back is a little weaker and has another element plus an ability. Your opponent sees the front and back elements of your remaining cards, never which side you'll play. Tap ↻ on a card in your collection to see its back.

5 · MANAGE YOUR 15 ENERGY

Every match you start with 15 energy. Playing a card spends its cost. If a card costs more than the energy you have left, it still plays, but exhausted at half power (and spends nothing).

Example: a deck with a legendary (6), an epic (4) and three rares (3+3+3) costs 19, four more than you have. One or two of those cards will play exhausted, so decide which round you can afford to weaken. A cheap deck of commons and rares never gets tired.

FINAL POWER

Power ×1.3 if your element wins ×0.5 if exhausted

  • Storm Eagle (wind, 52) vs Rock Tortoise (earth, 30): wind beats earth, so 52 × 1.3 = 68 vs 30. Eagle wins.
  • Ember Fox (fire, 28) vs Frost Whale (water, 50): water beats fire, so 28 vs 50 × 1.3 = 65. Whale wins.
  • Ashborn Phoenix (legendary, 94) played with only 4 energy left: exhausted, 94 × 0.5 = 47. It loses to a Lava Golem (48).

6 · ENTRY, POT AND FEES

  • When you search for a match, your entry is locked in escrow. Cancel the search and it's refunded.
  • The winner gets the pot (both entries) minus a 5% house fee (4% in the Legend room). Example in Rookie: pot 40,000, fee 2,000, you receive 38,000, a profit of 18,000.
  • The fee is split: 40% burned (removed from supply), 40% treasury, 20% season rewards.
  • A drawn match refunds both entries with no fee.

7 · GET MORE CARDS AND FLIP

  • New players start with 200,000 FLIP, 12 starter cards and 2 free packs.
  • Basic pack (15,000 FLIP): 3 cards, mostly commons with a 1% legendary chance. Premium pack (60,000 FLIP): 3 cards, all rare or better, 7% legendary.
  • Claim a daily bonus of 25,000 FLIP every 24 hours from the Arena.

8 · FUSE CARDS IN THE FORGE

Put 2–4 cards of the same rarity into the Forge and pay a small fee. You either get one card of the next rarity or, if the fusion fails, one random card of the same rarity back. Your input cards are used up either way.

9 · TRADE ON THE MARKET

List any card for a price in FLIP. Other players see it live and can buy it instantly. When it sells, the card moves to the buyer and you receive the price minus a 5% fee (half burned, half treasury). A listed card can't be used in decks or the Forge; cancel the listing any time to get it back. You always keep at least 5 unlisted cards.

10 · CLIMB THE WEEKLY SEASON

Everyone starts at 1200 ELO. Winning raises it and losing lowers it; beating a higher-rated player earns more. Your ELO sets your tier: Bronze, Silver (1150), Gold (1300), Platinum (1450), Diamond (1600), Master (1800).

Each season lasts 7 days. Play at least 3 ranked matches to qualify. The top 100 win FLIP: 1st 1,000,000 · 2nd 500,000 · 3rd 250,000, then 800,000 ÷ place (10th 80,000, 100th 8,000). The top 3 also earn a permanent badge. At the end of a season ratings move halfway back to 1200.

TIPS

  • Watch their element squares. If only water is left on their side, your fire cards are in trouble.
  • Don't open with your legendary if that leaves your later cards exhausted.
  • Losing a round on purpose with a cheap card can save energy for a big win later.
  • If you close the page mid-match, open it again: you rejoin the match. While you're away, random cards play for you.

FLIP and cards are in-game items with no cash value.
